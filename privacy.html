Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

Clay Health is a personal tool used only by its owner, Clayton Mergen. It requests read-only access to the owner's own sleep, activity and health measurement data from the Google Health API.

  • What is accessed: the owner's sleep, activity and heart rate data, read-only.
  • How it is used: only to create a private weekly summary emailed to the owner.
  • Sharing: data is not sold, shared or used for advertising. It is not transferred to anyone other than the owner.
  • Storage: credentials are kept in the owner's private environment settings. Summaries are kept in the owner's private storage.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
